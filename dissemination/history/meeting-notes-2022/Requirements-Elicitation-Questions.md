# Requirements Elicitation Questions

These questions have been prepared to be asked to the annotator for the meeting on 24.02.2022 14:00. Initial elicitation notes and observations of the software have been used.

# Questions

- In what circumstances, does the mouse need to be used?
- What are the dependency relationships in case of an _autocomplete_ feature?
- How many columns are needed on average?
- Would showing the rows of lemmas in a compact manner affect the experience badly?
- Does the software freeze up in a frequently-occurring scenario?
- Example of refresh needed but not done in the case of lemma update
- Examples of bad error messages and what the better ones would be
- Example of horizontal scroll while navigating
- How would 2-screen usage look like?
- Should the reset shortcut have a pop-up?
- How do _Comments_ and _Notes_ for an annotation differ?
- In case of a _search_ feature, what would be the frequent search types?

# Results

## Notes

- Column labels and buttons atop should be converted to a toolbar-like functionality, taking less space by default.
- Area is the key improvement needed.
- User preferences of column sizes, tree types, etc. would be convenient for different users.
- The sentence should be shown at all times.
- New keyboard shortcuts are needed, making the mouse unnecessary for all tasks.

Notes from the meeting can also be seen [here](https://gitlab.com/furkan.akkurt/web-annotation-tool/-/wikis/24.02.2022-14:00-Meeting-Notes).
