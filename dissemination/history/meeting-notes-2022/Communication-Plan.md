# Communication Plan

Meetings with the advisor every week are to be on Fridays after the usual DIP Meeting at 14:00 this semester (Spring 2022), unless otherwise specified. The meetings are conducted via Zoom.

Meetings with the annotator are to be conducted via Zoom at various times through the semester, whenever they are needed.

Slack is used frequently for textual discussions.

Meetings are to be documented on this [page](https://gitlab.com/furkan.akkurt/web-annotation-tool/-/wikis/Meetings).
